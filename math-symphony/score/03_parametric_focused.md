## 1. Basic Foundations (Expressions 1-15)
- **Logic:**
    - `(t, t)` (Diagonal)
    - `(t, sin(t))` (Sine wave)
    - `(cos(t), sin(t))` (Unit Circle)

- **Note:** Standard domains range from `2*pi` to `10*pi`.

## 2. Trigonometric Interference (Expressions 48-65)

## 3. Tangent & Secant Mesh (Expressions 70-100)
*Creating dense vertical and horizontal grids via asymptotic functions.*

- **Logic:**
    - `(tan(19.5t), sec(t) + sin(40t))`
    - `(tan(20.5t), sec(t) + sin(41t) * tan(t))`
    - `(tan(30t) + sin(t), sec(t) * sin(30t))`
    - `(tan(25t) + sin(99t), sec(50t) + sin(t))`
- **The "Crown" Expression:**
    - `(tan(t), csc(t) * tan(0.98t) - sin(t))` (Domain: `100*pi`)
